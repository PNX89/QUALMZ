500 days, generated from seed 20260829. No capital was ever allocated and neither
series is a record of anything traded.

                 mean diff   naive t   block t      alerts
  a bug           0.000480     18.24      4.00   naive and block
  bad luck       -0.000284     -3.26     -0.95   naive

The second row is two series with no drift between them at all, differing only by
noise that wanders. The naive standard error treats each day as an independent
observation and reports t = -3.26, which is a bug. Taking the standard error
across 25 non-overlapping blocks instead gives -0.95, which is a fortnight of bad weeks.
